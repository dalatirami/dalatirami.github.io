Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Welcome to Dalati English (accessible from our website). This Privacy Policy explains how we and our third-party advertising partners collect, use, process, and protect your personal data when you visit our site, in accordance with applicable privacy laws including the UK GDPR.

1. Information Collected

When you use Dalati English, information is collected both directly by us and automatically by third-party partners:

  • Directly Provided Data: When you contact us via email (info@dalati.org), we receive your name, email address, message contents, and any attachments.
  • Automated & Technical Data: Our servers and third-party advertising/analytics partners (such as Ezoic and Google) automatically collect technical data, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, device information, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, timestamps, and browsing activity. Under UK GDPR, IP addresses and device identifiers are considered personal data.

2. Why We Process Your Data (Legal Basis)

Under applicable data protection laws (such as the UK GDPR), we process personal data based on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: We rely on your explicit consent (obtained via our consent management platform) for setting non-essential cookies and for delivering personalized advertising.
  • Legitimate Interests: We process technical logs for site security, administration, and performance optimization. Our advertising partners may also rely on legitimate interests for non-personalized ad delivery and measurement, provided your fundamental rights do not override those interests.
  • Legitimate Interests / Legal Obligation: To respond to your direct inquiries, communicate with you, and comply with our legal requirements.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

First-Party Storage: We use browser localStorage (specifically the dalati-theme key) strictly to remember your visual preferences (Dark Mode or Light Mode).

Third-Party Cookies: Our advertising and analytics partners use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your device and browsing behavior for ad delivery, measurement, and site analytics.

Depending on your location, you can manage your cookie preferences through the site's consent settings dialogue or by modifying your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Technologies

Dalati English provides free educational resources. To support our site maintenance and content development, we currently integrate reputable third-party advertising networks, including Ezoic and Google AdSense.

Data Collection for Ads: These partners collect information regarding ad requests, impressions, clicks, and your interactions with advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and web beacons to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website or other sites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their browsing history.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' uses of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

To learn how Google manages privacy and data across its advertising products, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms Site.

5. Embedded Third-Party Content

Our articles may embed multimedia content (e.g., YouTube videos for pronunciation practice). When you interact with this embedded content, YouTube (Google LLC) may collect viewing metrics, log your IP address, and set third-party cookies or device identifiers in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

External educational links on our site (such as Ba Ba Dum) are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing and International Transfers

We share necessary data with our hosting providers and advertising partners (such as Ezoic and Google) to operate and monetize the site. Because some of our partners and infrastructure are located outside the UK and the European Economic Area (EEA), your data (such as IP addresses and cookie identifiers) may be transferred internationally. We ensure that these transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards required by applicable data protection law.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data provided via contact inquiries only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry. Technical logs and advertising-related identifiers are retained by our third-party partners according to their own retention policies, generally for a limited period necessary to fulfill advertising and analytics purposes.

8. User Rights (UK GDPR & CCPA Principles)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the UK, EEA, and California), you are entitled to specific rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation and copies of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of Rectification: Request that we update or correct inaccurate details.
  • Right of Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Object or Restrict Processing: Object to our processing (e.g., for direct marketing or legitimate interests) or request restrictions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time for activities reliant on consent (e.g., personalized ads) via our consent settings.
  • Right to Complain: Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK, if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise your rights directly with us, contact info@dalati.org. We respond to verified requests within statutory timeframes.

9. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. However, our third-party advertising partners may use automated profiling (based on your browsing habits and consent) to serve relevant advertisements.

10. Children's Privacy

Dalati English is an educational platform intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age limits) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information inappropriately, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our advertising setup, operational practices, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.

12. How to Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team:
