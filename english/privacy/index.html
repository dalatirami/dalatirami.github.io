Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

Welcome to dalati.org, encompassing Dalati English (educational platform and resources) as well as the official developer portfolio and web tools of Rami Dalati (accessible from dalati.org and Dalati English). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit dalati.org and its sections, how that information is used and shared, and the choices available to you. We handle personal data in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the UK GDPR where it applies.

1. Information Collected

When you use dalati.org or Dalati English, information is collected both directly by us and automatically by third-party partners:

  • Directly Provided Data: When you contact us via email (info@dalati.org), we receive your name, email address, message contents, and any attachments.
  • Automated & Technical Data: When you access the website, our hosting, security, and technical service providers may process technical information such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, device information, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring and exit pages, timestamps, and request data. Third-party services operating on a page, including advertising or analytics providers, may also collect technical information through their own technologies. Under the UK GDPR, IP addresses and certain device identifiers may constitute personal data.

2. Why We Process Your Data (Legal Basis)

Under applicable data protection laws (such as the UK GDPR), we process personal data based on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: We obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies, or processing personal data for advertising purposes, where applicable law requires it.
  • Legitimate Interests: We process technical logs for site security, administration, and performance optimization.
  • Legitimate Interests / Legal Obligation: To respond to your direct inquiries, communicate with you, and comply with our legal requirements.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

First-Party Storage: We use browser localStorage (such as dalati-theme and dalati-lang) strictly to remember your functional preferences (Dark Mode, Light Mode, and language selection). In our client-side web utilities (such as Dalati QR Tools), processing runs locally in your browser without transmitting your input content or files to our servers.

Third-Party Cookies: Third-party advertising and analytics providers may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar technologies to collect information about your device and activity on pages where their services operate. These technologies may be used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention, and analytics.

Where a consent-management interface is provided, you can use it to review or change the choices it offers. You can also manage or block cookies through your browser settings. For questions about privacy choices or to exercise applicable rights, contact us using the details below. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Technologies

dalati.org and Dalati English provide free educational resources, digital tools, and portfolio materials. Advertisements displayed on the website may be served by Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These providers may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver, measure, and improve advertising, subject to the applicable settings, user choices, and legal requirements.

Data Collection for Ads: In connection with advertising, Google and other participating advertising providers may process information such as IP address, browser and device information, page URL, ad requests, impressions, clicks, and interactions with advertisements. Each provider processes information under its own privacy documentation and terms.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements. These technologies may support personalized advertising based on visits to this and other websites, where permitted by applicable law and the user's consent choices.
  • Google's advertising technologies may use information from this and other websites to select and personalize ads, subject to the user's choices, applicable law, and Google's policies.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' uses of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

For details about information Google receives from websites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Our articles may embed multimedia content (e.g., YouTube videos for pronunciation practice). When you interact with this embedded content, YouTube (Google LLC) may collect viewing metrics, log your IP address, and set third-party cookies or device identifiers in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

External educational links on our site (such as Ba Ba Dum) are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing and International Transfers

We share necessary data with service providers that help us operate the website and its features. Uploaded files may be stored or handled using Supabase and Cloudflare R2 as applicable to the feature you use. These providers act as infrastructure or storage providers; file processing is performed by our own website/server-side code. We do not send uploaded files to external AI services for AI processing. If and when advertising is enabled, relevant technical and advertising data may be processed by advertising partners such as Google in connection with ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted. Because some providers and infrastructure may be located outside the UK and the European Economic Area (EEA), data may be transferred internationally, subject to safeguards required by applicable data protection law.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data provided via contact inquiries only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry. Where technical logs or advertising-related identifiers are processed by third-party providers, those providers retain them in accordance with their own retention policies.

8. User Rights Under Applicable Data Protection Laws

Depending on your location and the data protection laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning your personal data. The scope of these rights and the conditions for exercising them vary by jurisdiction:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation and copies of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of Rectification: Request that we update or correct inaccurate details.
  • Right of Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Object or Restrict Processing: Object to our processing (e.g., for direct marketing or legitimate interests) or request restrictions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time for activities that rely on consent, using the consent-management interface when it is available or by contacting us.
  • Right to Complain: Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK, if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise your rights directly with us, contact info@dalati.org. We respond to verified requests within statutory timeframes.
